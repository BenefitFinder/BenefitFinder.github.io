If you qualify as aged, blind or disabled and meet income/resource limits, you may be eligible for health care coverage under Apple Health (Washington’s Medicaid program) or other DSHS-administered medical assistance. This program ensures access to medical, dental, vision, and other needed services even if you can’t work or are on a fixed income.

---

---

### Additional Notes on Eligibility & Coverage

- The health care coverage for “aged, blind or disabled” is also known as “Classic Medicaid,” “Non-MAGI Medicaid,” or “SSI-Related Medicaid.”
- If you’re already receiving Medicare (e.g. because you’re over 65), Apple Health can act as secondary coverage, helping pay for costs Medicare doesn’t cover.
- Income and resource limits apply, and are reviewed annually.
- If your income or resources exceed the regular limits, there may still be options such as “spend-down” or other medical assistance programs.

    <ul>
      <li>State-issued ID or driver’s license</li>

      <li>Tribal ID</li>
      <li>Social Security card (or proof that you applied for one)</li>

 <details>
    <summary>2. Proof of Age</summary>
    <ul>
      <li>Birth certificate</li>
      <li>Driver’s license or state ID</li>
      <li>Passport</li>
      <li>Any official document listing your date of birth</li>
    </ul>
  </details>

  <details>
    <summary>3. Proof of Disability or Blindness</summary>
    <ul>
      <li>SSA disability award letter (if applicable)</li>
      <li>Medical records, doctor’s statements, or hospital records</li>
      <li>Physical, mental, or psychiatric evaluations</li>
      <li>Other medical documents showing the condition is expected to last 12+ months</li>
    </ul>
    <p>DSHS may request you complete additional evaluations with a contracted provider.</p>
  </details>

  <details>
    <summary>4. Proof of Washington Residency</summary>
    <ul>
      <li>Utility bills, lease or rental agreement, mortgage statement, or similar proof of address</li>
      <li>Any official mail showing name and current WA address</li>
    </ul>
  </details>

  <details>
    <summary>5. Proof of Income and Resources</summary>
    <ul>
      <li>Recent pay stubs or employer letters (if any income)</li>

      <li>Documentation of savings, assets, or other resources (if required under program rules)</li>
    </ul>
  </details>

    <ul>

    </ul>

### What Services & Benefits Are Provided

- Medical coverage for doctor visits, hospital care, mental health, prescriptions, dental, vision, and other care (depending on enrollment plan under Apple Health).
- For individuals 65+, blind, or disabled — including long-term care, long-term support, or home/community-based services when eligible. 
- If you have Medicare (because of age or disability), Apple Health can help with Medicare premiums, copays, and other medical costs that Medicare doesn’t fully cover.
