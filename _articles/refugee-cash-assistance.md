### Additional Notes on Eligibility
Exact eligibility criteria for RCA are slightly more complex. Here's a checklist for further criteria you can consult.
 - You must have been granted one of the following statuses:
	- Refugee
	- Asylee
 	- Amerasian
	- Cuban-Haitian entrant
	- Victim of trafficking or eligible family
	- Special Immigrant Visa holder from Irag or Afghanistan or eligible family
	- Afghan humanitarian parolees eligible for federal benefits
	- Ukranian humanitarian parolees eligible for federal benefits
 - You are not eligible for Temporary Assistance for Needy Families
 - You have not within the last 30 days willingly refused or quit employment despite being employable
 - Are not a full-time student at a college or university
 - Possess less than $12,000 (There are many exemptions to this, like a car, details are found [here](https://app.leg.wa.gov/WAC/default.aspx?cite=388-470-0045))

### Examples of Documents you will need:
<div class="faq-section">
	<details>
	  <summary>1. Proof of Income</summary>
	  <h4>Earned Income</h4>
	  <ul>
	  	<li>Pay stubs (1-3 months)</li>
	  	<li>Letter from employer</li>
	  	<li>Self-employment: tax return, invoices, or bookkeeping records</li>
	  </ul>
	  <h4>Unearned Income</h4>
	  <ul>
	  	<li>Unemployment benefits</li>
		<li>Worker's compensation</li>
		<li>Pension or retirement income</li>
		<li>Child support or alimony</li>
		<li>Bank statement showing desposits</li>
	  </ul>
	  If you are not considered an able bodied worker or unemployed you may still be eligible, In this case you might need to bring one or more of the following, depending on your situation:
	  <ul>
		<li>Proof of disability</li>
		<li>Proof of a dependent aged 14 or younger</li>
	  </ul>
	</details>

	<details>
	  <summary>2. Immigration Documentation</summary>
	  <ul>
		<li>Permanent resident card (Green Card)</li>
		<li>Name of resettlement agency</li>
		<li>I-94 or DHS/USCIS documents</li>
	  </ul>
	  <h4>Victims of Trafficking</h4>
	  <ul>
		<li>For adult victims, the original certification letter from the HHS</li>
		<li>For minor victims, the HHS issues a special eligibility or interim assistance letter</li>
		<li>For family, a T-2, T-3, T-4, T-5, or T-6 Visa</li>
	  </ul>
	</details>

	<details>
	  <summary>3. Washington Residency & Living Situation</summary>
	  <u1>
		<li>Lease, rental agreement, or mortgage statement</li>
		<li>Utility bills (electric, gas, water, phone, internet)</li>
		<li>Letter from landlord</li>
		<li>Letter from shelter or agency</li>
	  </u1>
	</details>
</div>
