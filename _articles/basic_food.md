requirements:
  - Social Security Number
  - Proof of Identity
  - Proof of Income (multiple forms is better)
  - Proof of Immigration status (if not a citizen)
  - Proof of Household Expenses

steps:
  - Gather your documents and information
  - Complete the application (online, phone, in person, or mail)
  - Complete the follow up interview
  - Submit verification documents
  - Wait for approval (typically within 30 days)
  - Collect your EBT card (mailed or picked up)
  - About 6 months after approval you will need to do a mid-certification reviewl
  - Towards the end of your approval period (typically 12 months) you will need to complete the eligibility review form

### Additional Notes on Eligibility
Determining exact eligibilty using our tool can be tough for SNAP. Here's a quick checklist you can consult to double check. If you meet all of these criteria there's a very good chance you are eligible.
- You live in Washington
- You’re a U.S. citizen or a qualifying immigrant
- Your gross monthly household income is around or below the amounts listed in the table below
- You do **not** fall under the strict student rule **OR** you meet a student exemption. These rules can be found [here](https://www.dshs.wa.gov/esa/eligibility-z-manual-ea-z/student-status)
- If you’re 18–52 with no kids, you are working, in training, or you meet an exemption

<br>

### Examples of Documents you will need:
<div class="faq-section">

  <details>
    <summary>1. Proof of Identity</summary>
    <ul>
      <li>State ID or driver’s license</li>
      <li>U.S. passport</li>
      <li>Birth certificate</li>
      <li>Work or school ID</li>
      <li>Tribal ID</li>
      <li>Any official document showing your name and identity</li>
    </ul>
  </details>

  <details>
    <summary>2. Social Security Number (SSN)</summary>
    <ul>
      <li>Social Security card</li>
      <li>Any document showing SSN (pay stub, SSA letter, tax form)</li>
      <li>If you do not have a SSN, you can still apply. DSHS will guide next steps.</li>
    </ul>
  </details>

  <details>
    <summary>3. Proof of Income</summary>
    <h4>Earned Income</h4>
    <ul>
      <li>Pay stubs (1–3 months)</li>
      <li>Letter from employer</li>
      <li>Self-employment: tax return, invoices, or bookkeeping records</li>
    </ul>
    <h4>Unearned Income</h4>
    <ul>
      <li>Social Security or SSI award letters</li>
      <li>Unemployment benefits</li>
      <li>Worker’s compensation</li>
      <li>Pension or retirement income</li>
      <li>Child support or alimony</li>
      <li>Bank statements showing deposits</li>
    </ul>
    If you are not considered an able bodied worker or unemployed you may still be eligible. In this case you might need to bring one or more of the following, depending on your situation:
    <ul>
      <li>Proof of disability</li>
      <li>Proof of a dependent aged 14 or younger</li>
      <li>Documents supporting enrollment in school</li>
    </ul>

  </details>

  <details>
    <summary>4. Immigration or Citizenship Status</summary>
    <ul>
      <li>Permanent resident card (Green Card)</li>
      <li>Employment Authorization Document</li>
      <li>I-94 or DHS/USCIS documents</li>
      <li>Proof of refugee or asylee status</li>
      <li>Only required for those applying for benefits</li>
    </ul>
  </details>

  <details>
    <summary>5. Washington Residency & Living Situation</summary>
    <ul>
      <li>Lease, rental agreement, or mortgage statement</li>
      <li>Utility bills (electric, gas, water, phone, internet)</li>
      <li>Letter from landlord</li>
      <li>Letter from shelter or agency</li>
    </ul>
  </details>

  <details>
    <summary>6. Household Expenses (Helps Determine Benefit Amount)</summary>
    <ul>
      <li>Rent or mortgage amount</li>
      <li>Utility costs</li>
      <li>Childcare or dependent care expenses</li>
      <li>Child support paid</li>
      <li>Health expenses for seniors (60+) or disabled household members</li>
      <li>Property taxes / homeowner’s insurance (if applicable)</li>
    </ul>
  </details>

  <details>
    <summary>7. Other Documents (Only If Requested)</summary>
    <ul>
      <li>Proof of job loss or reduced hours</li>
      <li>Proof someone moved in/out</li>
      <li>Sponsor income information (some immigrants)</li>
      <li>Letter explaining unusual income or living situations</li>
      <li>“Collateral contact” or someone DSHS can call to verify a detail</li>
    </ul>
  </details>

  <details>
    <summary>Expedited (Emergency) Basic Food</summary>
    <p>You may qualify for 7-day expedited service if your household has very little income or resources.</p>
    <p><strong>You only need proof of identity at first.</strong> Everything else can come later.</p>
  </details>

</div>
