The Housing and Essential Needs (HEN) Referral Program connects low-income Washington residents who cannot work due to a physical or mental incapacity with basic needs assistance, housing support, and utilities help. HEN is administered by the state through DSHS and local service-provider partners under the oversight of the Department of Commerce.

### Additional Notes on Eligibility & How HEN Works

- HEN is designed for adults who are unable to work for at least 90 days due to health-related physical or mental incapacity, and who are **not eligible** for <a href="/articles/aged-blind-disabled-cash.html">ABD cash</a> benefits.
- You must meet financial need criteria such as low income and limited resources per state rules.
- Even if you qualify for referral, receiving HEN services is not guaranteed and actual support depends on availability of funds and a local provider’s assessment.
- HEN services may include rent payment or arrears, utility assistance, security or utility deposits, and relocation costs (if homeless or at risk).
- HEN also provides non-cash essential-needs suppor such as personal hygiene items, cleaning supplies, transportation assistance, and basic necessities.

### Important Limitations & Notes

- HEN referral does **not guarantee** housing or rental assistance — funding is limited and subject to availability.
- If you are eligible for other higher-priority benefits (e.g. TANF, SSI), you may be disqualified from HEN.
- You must stay below income and resource thresholds, report changes in circumstances, and complete eligibility reviews as required. 
- HEN is meant as a short-term or emergency support. It is not guaranteed long-term housing or ongoing rent support.
