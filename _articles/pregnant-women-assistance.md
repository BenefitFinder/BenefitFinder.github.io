The Pregnant Women Assistance (PWA) Program provides temporary cash assistance, up to $450 per month, to pregnant individuals in Washington who have low income and do **not** qualify for other programs such as TANF, SFA, or SSI. This program is intended to support pregnant individuals throughout pregnancy until delivery.

### Contact Information
- [Find a local DSHS office](https://www.dshs.wa.gov/office-locations)  
- **DSHS Customer Service Contact Center:** 877-501-2233  
- **Email:** AskDSHS@dshs.wa.gov  

---

### Additional Notes on Eligibility

PWA is a *last-resort* cash program. You may qualify if all of the following apply:

- You are currently pregnant  
- You meet Washington income limits for the program  
- You are a U.S. citizen or a qualified immigrant 
- You are **not eligible** for:
  - TANF (Temporary Assistance for Needy Families)
  - SFA (State Family Assistance)
  - SSI (Supplemental Security Income)
- If DSHS determines it’s needed, you complete or participate in a substance use disorder evaluation/treatment program
- You live in Washington

If all of these apply, you likely qualify for PWA.

<br>

### Income Guidelines
Income limits for PWA follow Washington State cash assistance standards. These may change each year. As of 2025, most applicants qualify if their income is below approximately 62% of the federal poverty level, after countable deductions.

*Exact limits depend on your situation and DSHS will determine eligibility.*

### Notes About the Program

- PWA provides cash assistance only. It does *not* include ongoing support services.  
- If you later become eligible for TANF, SFA, or SSI, PWA will generally end.  
- After delivery, you may transition to State Family Assistance or TANF, depending on eligibility.  
- Some requirements vary depending on immigration category and household situation.
