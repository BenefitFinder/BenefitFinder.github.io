### Additional Notes on Eligibility

Here’s a more in-depth checklist commonly used to determine whether someone is likely eligible for AEM:

- To qualify for an alien medical program (AMP) a person must: 
- Be ineligible for federally funded Washington apple health (WAH) programs due to the citizenship/alien status requirements described in <a href="https://app.leg.wa.gov/WAC/default.aspx?cite=182-503-0535">WAC 182-503-0535</a>.
- Meet the requirements described in <a href="https://app.leg.wa.gov/WAC/default.aspx?cite=182-507-0115">WAC 182-507-0115</a>, <a href="https://app.leg.wa.gov/WAC/default.aspx?cite=182-507-0120">WAC 182-507-0120</a>, or <a href="https://app.leg.wa.gov/WAC/default.aspx?cite=182-507-0125">WAC 182-507-0125</a>; and
- Meet all categorical and financial eligibility criteria for one of the following programs, except for the Social Security number or citizenship/alien status requirements: 
- An SSI-related medical program described in chapters <a href="https://app.leg.wa.gov/WAC/default.aspx?cite=182-511">182-511</a> and <a href="https://app.leg.wa.gov/WAC/default.aspx?cite=182-512">182-512</a> WAC;
- A MAGI-based program referred to in <a href="https://app.leg.wa.gov/WAC/default.aspx?cite=182-503-0510">WAC 182-503-0510</a>; or
- The breast and cervical cancer treatment program for women described in <a href="https://app.leg.wa.gov/WAC/default.aspx?cite=182-505-0120">WAC 182-505-0120</a>; or
- A medical extension described in <a href="https://app.leg.wa.gov/WAC/default.aspx?cite=182-523-0100">WAC 182-523-0100</a>.
- AMP medically needy (MN) health care coverage is available only for children, pregnant women and persons who meet SSI-related criteria. See <a href="https://app.leg.wa.gov/WAC/default.aspx?cite=182-519-0100">WAC 182-519-0100</a> for MN eligibility and <a href="https://app.leg.wa.gov/WAC/default.aspx?cite=182-519-0110">WAC 182-519-0110</a> for spending down excess income under the MN program.
- The agency or its designee does not consider a person's date of arrival in the United States when determining eligibility for AMP.
- For non-MAGI-based programs, the agency or its designee does not consider a sponsor's income and resources when determining eligibility for AMP, unless the sponsor makes the income or resources available. Sponsor deeming does not apply to MAGI-based programs.
- A person is not eligible for AMP if that person entered the state specifically to obtain medical care.
- A person who the agency or its designee determines is eligible for AMP may be eligible for retroactive coverage as described in <a href="https://app.leg.wa.gov/WAC/default.aspx?cite=182-504-0005">WAC 182-504-0005</a>.
- Once the agency or its designee determines financial and categorical eligibility for AMP, the agency or its designee then determines whether a person meets the requirements described in <a href="https://www.hca.wa.gov/free-or-low-cost-health-care/i-help-others-apply-and-access-apple-health/wac-182-507-0115-alien-emergency-medical-program-aem">WAC 182-507-0115</a>, <a href="https://www.hca.wa.gov/free-or-low-cost-health-care/i-help-others-apply-and-access-apple-health/wac-182-507-0120-alien-medical-dialysis-and-cancer-treatment-and-treatment-life-threatening-benign-tumors">WAC 182-507-0120</a>, or <a href="https://www.hca.wa.gov/free-or-low-cost-health-care/i-help-others-apply-and-access-apple-health/wac-182-507-0125-state-funded-long-term-care-services">WAC 182-507-0125</a>.

### Examples of Documents You May Need

<div class="faq-section">

  <details>
    <summary>1. Proof of Identity / Social Security Number</summary>
    <ul>
      <li>State-issued ID or driver’s license</li>
      <li>U.S. passport</li>
      <li>Tribal ID</li>
      <li>Social Security card (or proof that you applied for one)</li>
    </ul>
  </details>

 <details>
    <summary>2. Proof of Age</summary>
    <ul>
      <li>Birth certificate</li>
      <li>Driver’s license or state ID</li>
      <li>Passport</li>
      <li>Any official document listing your date of birth</li>
    </ul>
  </details>

  <details>
    <summary>3. Proof of Disability or Blindness</summary>
    <ul>
      <li>SSA disability award letter (if applicable)</li>
      <li>Medical records, doctor’s statements, or hospital records</li>
      <li>Physical, mental, or psychiatric evaluations</li>
      <li>Other medical documents showing the condition is expected to last 12+ months</li>
    </ul>
    <p>DSHS may request you complete additional evaluations with a contracted provider.</p>
  </details>

  <details>
    <summary>4. Proof of Washington Residency</summary>
    <ul>
      <li>Utility bills, lease or rental agreement, mortgage statement, or similar proof of address</li>
      <li>Any official mail showing name and current WA address</li>
    </ul>
  </details>

  <details>
    <summary>5. Proof of Income and Resources</summary>
    <ul>
      <li>Recent pay stubs or employer letters (if any income)</li>
      <li>Bank statements, benefit award letters (Social Security, pension, etc.)</li>
      <li>Documentation of savings, assets, or other resources (if required under program rules)</li>
    </ul>
  </details>

  <details>
    <summary>6. Proof of Immigration or Citizenship Status (if not a U.S. citizen)</summary>
    <ul>
      <li>Permanent Resident Card (Green Card)</li>
      <li>USCIS / I-94 documents or other eligible immigration status verification</li>
    </ul>
    <p>Only required if not a U.S. citizen.</p>
  </details>

</div>
