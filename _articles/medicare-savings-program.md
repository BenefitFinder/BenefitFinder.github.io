The Medicare Savings Program (MSP) helps eligible low-income Medicare beneficiaries in Washington cover Medicare costs that can otherwise be expensive including premiums, deductibles, and copays. MSP is a state-managed program (administered by Washington State Health Care Authority / Washington State Department of Social and Health Services) that supplements federal Medicare for people with limited income.  

Depending on income level and circumstances, MSP offers different levels of assistance under several sub-programs (tiers):



### What MSP Covers: Program Tiers & Benefits

| MSP Tier | What is Paid / Covered |
|----------|------------------------|
| **Qualified Medicare Beneficiary (QMB)** | Pays Medicare Part A & Part B premiums, deductibles, copayments and coinsurance (except prescription drug copays). |
| **Specified Low‑Income Medicare Beneficiary (SLMB)** | Pays Part B premiums only. |
| **Qualified Individual (QI‑1)** | Pays Part B premiums only (for individuals who meet income limits but exceed other tiers). |
| **Qualified Disabled Working Individual (QDWI)** | Pays Part A premiums (for working disabled under certain conditions). |

> **Note:** As of January 1, 2023, Washington no longer uses a resource / asset test for MSP and now eligibility depends only on income.

### Additional Notes on Eligibility & Process

- You must be “entitled to Medicare Part A” to qualify (having Part A is a prerequisite).
- MSP uses income limits, not resource/asset limits, to determine eligibility.
- Married couples or individuals living with a spouse are evaluated together in certain cases and spouse income may affect eligibility.
- There is no face-to-face interview requirement for MSP applications or renewals.

### Program Disclaimer  
MSP is administered by the Washington State Health Care Authority and DSHS. Eligibility and benefit levels may change over time. Always refer to the official MSP page for the latest rules and income thresholds.
